Checking a download, and first launch

Downloads come straight from GitHub Releases. Each file is listed in SHA256SUMS.txt and carries a build provenance attestation, a signed public record of the exact build that made it. Check one with gh attestation verify <file> --repo gceoale/goldfelty-portfolio-manager.

Builds aren’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer may warn you the first time you open Goldfelty. Is Goldfelty safe? explains why, how to check a download, and how to open it.

Questions, answered.

Is Goldfelty legit and safe?

Yes. Goldfelty is an open-source portfolio tracker, not a wallet. It never asks for your recovery phrase or private keys, never asks you to approve token spending, and can’t move funds without your own wallet asking you first. Every download is built from the public source code on GitHub and can be verified. Is Goldfelty safe? covers the details.

What is Goldfelty?

Goldfelty is a free crypto portfolio tracker for your desktop. It connects the wallets you already use and shows your total balance, its history and every holding across all your wallets and networks in one place. You can also send and receive from those wallets without switching apps.

Is Goldfelty a wallet? Can it see my private keys?

No. Goldfelty never creates a wallet and never asks for a recovery phrase or private key, because it never needs one. It reads your addresses, and every transfer is approved in your own wallet, such as MetaMask, Phantom or your Ledger.

Which wallets and networks does it support?

Over 35 browser wallets, including MetaMask, Phantom, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Trust Wallet, Solflare, UniSat, Xverse, Leather and TronLink. Ledger, Trezor and GridPlus hardware wallets connect through Frame, and phone wallets through WalletConnect. You can send on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Bitcoin, Solana and Tron, and track Litecoin, Dogecoin and XRP balances.

Can I track a wallet that won’t connect, like Exodus?

Yes. Add its addresses and Goldfelty tracks the balances so they count in your total. You send from that wallet itself.

How much does Goldfelty cost?

Downloading and using Goldfelty is free: tracking, charts, history and receiving cost nothing, and there are no ads or subscriptions. A fixed 0.5% fee applies only to tokens you send through Goldfelty, shown before you confirm. Sending from your wallet directly stays free.

Does it work on Mac, Windows and Linux?

Yes. There are builds for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, Windows on x64 and ARM (installer or portable), and Linux as an AppImage, .deb, .rpm or .tar.gz. Each one updates itself in the same form you installed.

Is my data private?

Your wallets, balances and history stay on your computer, encrypted with your password. There’s no analytics, and no account is needed. Balances are read from public blockchain services, which you can swap for your own node in Settings. The Privacy Policy lists everything the app contacts.

Is Goldfelty open source?

Yes. The source code is on GitHub, along with a security review. Every download is listed with its checksum and a signed record of the build that produced it.
