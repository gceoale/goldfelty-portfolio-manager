What it does

Connect what you already have

WalletConnect reaches wallets on your phone or this machine. Browser extensions connect through a page served on loopback. Or let Goldfelty make new smart accounts from one recovery phrase.

One view, many chains

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP, Polygon, BNB and Avalanche. Balances pooled by token, growth over time, allocation at a glance.

You pick the route

When several wallets hold the same token, Goldfelty asks which one pays instead of guessing. Same on the receiving side, before an address is ever copied.

Keys stay where they are

Connected wallets sign in their own app. Wallets made here are encrypted with scrypt and AES-256-GCM, and the recovery phrase is shown exactly once.

Before you put money in it

Goldfelty has not had an independent security audit, and the builds are not signed by Apple or Microsoft. It is worth a look and worth testing; it is not yet worth your savings. The source is public — read it before you trust it.
