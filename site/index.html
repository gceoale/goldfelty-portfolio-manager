Every wallet you use, in one desktop app.

Goldfelty shows what you hold across MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Frame, your phone wallet and the rest, what it’s worth, and how that changes. Send and receive from one place. Your wallets keep their keys and approve every transfer.

Download All platforms

Free for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Connect, don’t create

Goldfelty never makes a wallet or sees a key. It connects to the ones you have, and each asks you before it shares an account.

Its own connections

Browser wallets are reached through a tab on your own computer, and Frame over its local connection. Only phone wallets go through WalletConnect, because a phone can’t reach your computer directly.

You choose the wallet

When several wallets hold the same token, Goldfelty asks which one sends it. When receiving, you pick the wallet and network before an address is shown.

Checked before and after

It switches your wallet to the right network before asking, warns about lookalike addresses, and compares what the chain recorded with what you approved.

What it costs

The app is free. Goldfelty takes a fee on tokens you send through it, currently 0.5%, paid on top of the amount you send. The fee is shown on the review screen before anything is sent, and your wallet asks you to approve it as its own transaction. The owner of goldfelty.com can set the fee anywhere from 0% to 10%; see the Terms.
