Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

On your computer

The app stores your connected wallets’ addresses, labels, balances, history and settings on your computer, encrypted with a key protected by your password. It never stores private keys or recovery phrases, because it never has them.

What we receive

WhenWhat goldfelty.com receives
AlwaysOnly a request for the current fee rate (goldfelty.com/fees.json), which carries nothing about you beyond the IP address any web request shows. The app has no analytics or crash reporting.
You create an accountYour username, email address, one wallet address and a signature from that wallet proving you control it.
You turn on “Share my portfolio total”Your total portfolio value, its currency and how many wallets it spans, at most every six hours. Never individual addresses or holdings.
Any request to the account serviceYour IP address, kept only as a one-way hash in short-lived rate-limit counters that are regularly deleted.

How we use it

Account details reserve your username and let us contact you about your account. Shared totals let the Owner see how much value Goldfelty’s users manage, in aggregate and per account. We don’t sell any of it, and we don’t share it except with the hosting providers below or where the law requires.

Fees are public

Fee payments, like every blockchain transaction, are public on the network they’re sent on, and show your address sending to the Owner’s fee address.

Services involved

goldfelty.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and its account service on Cloudflare. The app also contacts blockchain RPC providers to read balances, CoinGecko for prices, and WalletConnect if you connect a phone wallet. Each of those sees your IP address and the requests the app makes to it. You can point the app at your own node in Settings.

Your choices

  • Turn sharing off in Settings. That deletes the total we hold, straight away.
  • Ask for your account and everything linked to it to be deleted at support@goldfelty.com.
  • Use the app without an account at all.
